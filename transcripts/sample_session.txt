Coaching Session - Sample Transcript
Date: March 27, 2026
Client: [Client Name]

Coach: Let's start with where you are right now. What's the biggest thing on your mind?

Client: I feel like I'm doing everything right but not seeing results. I'm working harder than ever but it feels like I'm running in place.

Coach: That's a really common place to get stuck. Here's what I want you to think about — are you busy or are you productive? Because there's a huge difference. A hamster on a wheel is working incredibly hard. Maximum effort. But it's going nowhere because the wheel isn't connected to anything.

Client: That's exactly what it feels like. A hamster wheel.

Coach: So the question isn't how hard are you working — it's what is your work actually connected to? What outcome does it produce? Let's look at your week. Walk me through what you spent the most time on.

Client: Mostly email, a lot of meetings, some client calls, and trying to prep for a big presentation next month.

Coach: Okay. Of those, which one directly moves the needle on your most important goal right now?

Client: Honestly... probably the client calls.

Coach: Right. Everything else is infrastructure — it has to happen, but it's not the engine. So the action item is this: next week I want you to protect three 90-minute blocks that are exclusively for client-facing work. No email, no prep meetings — just the actual work that produces results.

Client: That feels hard but I know I need to do it.

Coach: Hard is the point. If it were easy you'd already be doing it. The discomfort you feel about protecting that time is exactly why it matters. Comfort is the enemy of growth.

Client: I've been thinking about the fear piece too. I'm scared to say no to people.

Coach: Fear of saying no is usually fear of what people will think of you. And here's the truth — most people respect boundaries more than they respect doormats. When you say yes to everything, people stop trusting your yes because they know it doesn't mean anything.

Client: I never thought about it that way.

Coach: Your no makes your yes valuable. That's the reframe. It's not about being difficult — it's about being someone whose word means something.

Client: That's a really powerful way to look at it.

Coach: So two commitments from today: protect those three blocks, and practice saying no to one thing this week that you would normally say yes to out of obligation. Not fear. Deal?

Client: Deal.
